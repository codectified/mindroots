`src/components/reader/MobileQuranReader.module.css` adapt the layout and
styling of quran.com's translation view (`TranslationViewCell`, `VerseText`,
`QuranWord`, `InlineWordByWord`, `VerseLink`, `Separator`), from
https://github.com/quran/quran.com-frontend-next (referenced at commit
`aff1a035b0`, 2026-03-02).
